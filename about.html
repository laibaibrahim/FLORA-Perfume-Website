Our beginning

A love of the little things

FLORA began with a simple fascination: how a single scent can bring a place, a person, or a treasured afternoon vividly back to life.

Inspired by gardens, treasured keepsakes and the beauty of everyday rituals, we create fragrances that feel considered, expressive and wonderfully personal.

Our philosophy

Wear what feels like you

We believe a fragrance should invite discovery, not dictate identity. The best scent is the one that feels at home on your skin.

That is why every FLORA composition balances character with ease: expressive enough to be remembered, thoughtful enough to be worn every day.
