The fragrance story

Three notes, one beautiful journey.

First impression

Top notes

The bright opening of a fragrance. Citrus, herbs and light fruits often appear first and introduce the scent.

The heart

Middle notes

As the opening softens, floral, aromatic or spicy heart notes emerge and give a fragrance its character.

The lasting trail

Base notes

Woods, musk and amber provide depth, warmth and the soft impression that remains on skin.

Find your fragrance family

What are you drawn to?

There is no wrong answer. Follow the notes you naturally love.

Floral

Petals, bouquets and soft green stems. From fresh rose to creamy white flowers.

Fruity

Juicy, bright and gently sweet. Think peach, pear and sun-ripened citrus.

Woody

Grounded and comforting, with notes like sandalwood, cedar and vetiver.

Fresh & aromatic

Airy herbs, crisp citrus and clean accords with an easy, uplifting feel.

Warm & spicy

Soft amber, cardamom and gentle spice for a richer, enveloping impression.

Musk

Subtle and close to skin, musk can add softness and a quietly intimate finish.

A simple way to explore

Give a fragrance time.

Try it on skin and notice how it changes over a few hours. Your skin, the weather and the moment all play a part in how a fragrance feels.
